How it works

The technology behind the precision

  1. Laser beam emissionThe scanner emits a safe laser beam toward a surface.
  2. Vertical sweepA high-speed internal mirror spins and deflects the laser beam across a vertical plane, covering the space from top to bottom.
  3. Horizontal rotationAt the same time, the whole scanner body rotates 360° on its axis, ensuring complete coverage of the surrounding space.
  4. Point calculationFor each of the millions of laser pulses, the sensor measures the distance precisely. The result is a “point cloud”, where every point has accurate X, Y and Z coordinates.
Result

A faithful, complete 3D reconstruction of the space, ready to become intelligence for your project.
